Why you'll feel at home with us

Location

A 24/7 convenience store right on the ground floor, the new city promenade by the sea is a few steps away — and within reach you'll find a drugstore, pharmacy, restaurants, cafés and the popular Beer House.

Amenities

Fully equipped apartment for a comfortable stay — dishwasher, washing machine, iron with ironing board, hairdryer, microwave and a Nespresso capsule coffee machine. The living-room sofa folds out into two extra full sleeping spots, and multi-split air conditioning lets you set the temperature separately in every room. Guests also get a detailed apartment guide.

View

Sea, sunset and the new boulevard right from the balcony — see the real view in the gallery.

Peace and privacy

We don't rent the apartment out en masse. If you're staying with us, you're at home.

Active stay

A covered tennis court right on the building's premises, padel courts nearby, and a promenade for walking or cycling — sport and relaxation within reach.
